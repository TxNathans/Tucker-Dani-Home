## How to use this folder
- `BRIEFING.md` is the full working knowledge (version 2, November 13, 2026). Read it before answering any TRACER question. Treat it as background, not a task.
- `UPDATES.md` logs what changed after the briefing date. Newer entries win over the briefing. If Tucker says something that does not match either file, believe Tucker, ask what changed, and offer to add it to `UPDATES.md`.
- `business/` holds the business model, pricing, ideas, and `PROJECT-SETUP.md` (how to set up the Tracer BPA project on claude.ai). When Tucker brainstorms, give a recommendation, show the math, keep facts and ideas apart, and offer to log decisions in these files.
- The code, live site and swing data live on Tucker's lab laptop and the TRACER server, not here. From here: explain, plan, draft copy, read numbers Tucker pastes in, and write change requests (what, where, why, what done looks like) for the lab-laptop session to build.
