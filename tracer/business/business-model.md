# TRACER BPA business model

Working document. Facts come from `../BRIEFING.md` (November 13, 2026). Anything marked **Open** is undecided and is for brainstorming, not a decision.

## What TRACER sells
A hitting evaluation. The hitter swings on force plates with high-speed cameras running, and TRACER turns the recording into the Tracer Baseline Report: what already works, the one thing to fix first, why, a suggested fix (on the plates and off them), a 4-week plan and a retest.

Two delivery styles today:
- **Full report**: the complete evaluation, interactive page plus PDF.
- **Lesson plan**: a 30 to 45 minute lesson built from 2 to 3 swings, with an end-of-lesson retest.

## What makes it different
- Ground forces measured under each foot, plus pressure, video and 3D body and bat tracking, in one report.
- Every finding comes with a fix, on the plates and off them.
- Honest: plates prove, video suggests, nothing invented.
- A retest that shows whether the number moved, on the same bat.
- Athlete Assessment tied back to the swing.

## Known limits that affect the model
- Everyone with a login sees the same roster. Facility-by-facility privacy is not built, so it cannot yet be sold to a second facility as a private service.
- Reports build on Tucker's laptop. Throughput depends on it being on.
- The name has had no attorney check (TRACER Fitness was flagged). No trade-name filing is on record under Tucker's LLC.
