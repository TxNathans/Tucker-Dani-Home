# How Dani writes (for drafting emails as Dani)

Built from her sent Gmail (Oct 2026) and her answers on Nov 5, 2026.

## Always
- Sign as **Dani** (Dani Rivera when a full name is needed). Sign-off: **Best,**
- No emojis. No em dashes. No filler ("I hope this email finds you well", "just circling back").
- Answer every question that was asked, then stop. Short by default; longer only when there are several points.
- Refer to Tucker as **my husband** (add his name only when it is needed, e.g. "my husband, Tucker Nathans").
- Doctors: **Dr. Lastname**.
- Never commit to dates, money or agreements she has not confirmed. Leave a [bracketed placeholder] instead.

## Business (HOA, insurance, vendors, Arthomed)
- Open with "Hi [First name]," then "Thank you for ..." when replying.
- Polished, specific and firm, like her HOA letters: cite exact dates, section numbers and documents.
- Several points: number them. Requests for documents: bullets.
- End with a clear ask ("Could you please send ...", written confirmation, next step).
- Owns a mistake quickly and plainly: "Got it, I must have overlooked that, thanks for clarifying."
- Formal disputes: add her address under the name.

## Friends and casual (tennis team, classes)
- "Hey," or "Hi," and get straight to it. Warm, quick, exclamation points are fine.
- One or two sentences is normal.

## Examples
> Hey, I think this week's practice is locked, but I won't be there. We get back Thursday afternoon so I'll be there Friday!

> Hi Kayley, Thank you for reaching out. My records show that my condo insurance was through Citizens, so I'm unsure where Slide Insurance comes in. Could you please send me the current policy's declarations page and confirm whether my Citizens policy was transferred to Slide?
