## Checklist items not in the ChatGPT packet
- [ ] **Florida marriage license timing.** Florida residents have a 3-day waiting period after applying unless both finish a premarital course. To marry Fri Oct 16, apply at the Palm Beach County Clerk by about Mon Oct 12, or sooner. Confirm with the clerk.
- [ ] **Passports.** Both need valid passports for Mexico. Check expiration dates.
- [ ] **Names on tickets.** Book flights in the name on each passport. Dani's name won't be legally changed by Oct 17, so use her current passport name.
- [ ] **Same-day certificate copy.** Ask the clerk if a certified copy is available right after the ceremony, and ask Excellence if that copy (or a photo of it) is enough.
